## This stretch

- **"That many" for mills and discards** (D1280), and the questions asked before a resolution now
  see the event's amount and X (D1281). Both were real bugs: the context those questions were asked
  in was missing the numbers.
- **"That creature" in the other half of a sentence** (D1282).
- **A permanent's own enters and dies triggers that name another object** (D1283).
- **Enters with X counters**, where X is a count (D1284).
- **"Each other <kind> you control enters with an additional +1/+1 counter"** (D1285), and Metallic
  Mimic's "of the chosen type" (D1286).
- **Casting from the graveyard by permission** (D1287): Karador, Ghost Chieftain and its kin. A new
  engine capability: a permission, its once-a-turn use, the cast action naming it, and the bot
  using it.
- **Muldrotha, the Gravetide** (D1288): one permanent spell of each type a turn, and a land.
- **Kess, Dissident Mage; Edgar, Master Machinist; Overflow Submatrix** (D1289): the permission with
  its rider (exiled afterwards, enters tapped, enters with a finality counter).
- **Imprisoned in the Moon** (D1290), In Too Deep, Sugar Coat, Minimus Containment: a permanent made
  into a colorless land or artifact with one ability. On the way, an effect that takes every ability
  away and grants one keeps its own grant, and the mana and activation lists agree with that. An
  imprisoned Forest taps for {C}, not {G}.
- **Neoform** (D1291).
- **Enigmatic Incarnation and Vivien on the Hunt's +2** (D1292): a sacrifice made by the effect,
  then a search one mana value up from it.

Fixes found on the way:
- D1289's first draft read Overflow Submatrix's rider as "enters tapped". The probe showed the card
  unread, and its text is a finality counter.
- D1290's first fixture printed "Enchant creature or planeswalker" for the Clue Aura. The Aura fell
  off once its host stopped being a creature, which the rules require (CR 704.5m), and is why the
  real card prints "or Clue".
- D1292's search read the sacrifice's answer as the card it found, and returned the sacrificed
  enchantment from the graveyard. The exploratory test showed it before any assertion was written.
