## This stretch

- **The Spheres** (D1308): "If a red source would deal damage to you, prevent 2 of that damage",
  and its kin for any source, an opponent's source, a creature or an artifact. Several add up.
- **Keeping a permanent tapped** (D1309): "You may choose not to untap ~" is now its controller's
  standing choice. It is a setting on the card's sheet, not a play, so auto-pass is unaffected.
  The five storage lands that charge only while tapped now read whole. The bot never sets it.
- **A delayed draw from a permanent's ability** (D1310): Carrier Pigeons and the Mirage and Ice Age
  cantrip permanents.
- **Mana abilities with a second effect** (D1311): Chromatic Sphere, the Eggs and Barbed Sextant.
  These had been read as abilities that use the stack. They are now mana abilities (CR 605.1a):
  no stack, and the draw happens with the mana. Coverage did not move, because the cards were
  already read, wrongly.
- **X mana of any one color for X counters** (D1312): Petalmane Baku, Haruspex.
- **Coloured cost increases** (D1313): the Leeches' "{W} more". The surcharge is applied by every
  cost path, and the play sheet shows "+{W}" beside the printed cost.
- **The Ordeals** (D1314): "Then if it has three or more +1/+1 counters on it, sacrifice ~".
- **Colour changes** (D1315): "becomes the color of your choice" and "becomes blue until end of
  turn". This covers Wild Mongrel, Rainbow Crow, Spiritmonger and the Wisps.
- **Creature-type changes** (D1316): the Mistform cycle.
