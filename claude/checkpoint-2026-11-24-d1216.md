## What the engine learned since D1198

Measures and scopes:
- **Colours among a scope, differently named, the highest mana value among any scope** (D1199).
- **"Each [other] creature [you control] gets …"** read as the plural it is (D1200), with a guard
  where "it" refers to each creature in turn.
- **A group that can't block this turn**, and "creatures **without** flying" as a scope (D1208).

Triggers:
- **"One or more <kind> you control deal combat damage to a player"** (D1201): the batched hit
  now carries the creatures in it.
- **A tribe entering or dying with nobody's side named** (D1213): Elvish Vanguard.
- **A return from the graveyard on another card's death** (D1207): Nether Traitor.
- **A cast trigger that copies at a price** (D1210): Riku of Two Reflections, Mirari.

Sentences:
- **"If …, this spell costs {N} less to cast"** (D1202): the Avatar cycle.
- **"A. If …, B instead."** (D1203), and **B referring back to A's target** (D1204): Court of Ire,
  Frost Bite, Galvanize.
- **"Choose a color."** spells (D1206): Brave the Elements, Sudden Demise.
- **A pump and unblockable in one sentence** (D1214): Elusive Spellfist, Taigam's Strike.
- **A hand and graveyard shuffled into the library** (D1215): Timetwister, Echo of Eons.

Engine additions:
- **Protection from a colour as a grant** (D1205).
- **Mana in any combination of colours** (D1209): Terrarion, Smokebraider.
- **Switching power and toughness** in layer 7d (D1211): Aquamoeba, About Face.
- **Damage that can't be prevented, and players who can't gain life this turn** (D1212):
  Skullcrack, Combust.
- **Targets from a single graveyard** (D1216): Rapid Decay, Scarab Feast; the bot respects it.
