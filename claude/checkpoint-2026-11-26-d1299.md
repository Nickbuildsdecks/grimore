## This stretch

- **The web table was dropping five cast flags** (D1297): impending, a printed alternative cost,
  fuse, aftermath and a graveyard permission. The engine offered those plays and then refused
  what the table sent back, or charged the printed cost. Engine tests and the bot never showed it,
  because they build their own actions. A human tester would have hit it on their first Karador,
  fuse card or Snuff Out. Found while reading how the table sends special actions. A field-by-field
  check against the client found no others.
- **Rooms** (D1298): two doors, each unlocked on its own. Casting a door unlocks it, and its "When
  you unlock this door" fires. A locked door can be unlocked as a sorcery for its cost; the bot does
  this and the table offers it. Each door's abilities work only while it is unlocked. 27 of the 30
  Rooms play; 3 have a door rule that cannot be scoped to its door yet.
- **Eerie** (D1299): "whenever an enchantment you control enters and whenever you fully unlock a
  Room". 12 of 17 read whole. "Eerie" had been misfiled as a keyword, which kept every Eerie line
  unread.

Stated simplifications for Rooms: on the battlefield a Room's name, mana value and colours are the
whole card's, where the rules count only its unlocked doors.
