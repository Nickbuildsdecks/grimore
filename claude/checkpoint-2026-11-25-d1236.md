## Commander staples this stretch

This stretch went after cards a Commander table meets often and the engine could not run at all:

- **Strionic Resonator and its kin** (D1225): copying an ability on the stack, read from the card.
- **Null Rod, Stony Silence, Collector Ouphe, Cursed Totem, Linvala, Damping Matrix** (D1226):
  activated abilities that can't be activated, mana abilities included where the card says so.
- **Torpor Orb, Hushbringer** (D1227): entries and deaths that cause no triggers.
- **Panharmonicon, Teysa Karlov, Isshin, Elesh Norn** (D1228): triggers that trigger again, and
  Norn's silence for opponents.
- **Grand Arbiter Augustin IV, Reality Shift** (D1229).
- **Winter Orb, Static Orb** (D1230): limits on untapping. The engine picks which permanents untap,
  mana sources first; asking the player is not built yet.
- **Ethersworn Canonist** (D1231), and the bot no longer loops on a free grant to itself (Mist
  Dragon, found by sweep 84001).
- **Stranglehold, Mindlock Orb, Aven Mindcensor** (D1232): searches stopped or limited, and
  Stranglehold's skipped extra turns.
- **Narset's and Spirit of the Labyrinth's draw limit** (D1233), applied before Notion Thief.
- **Smokestack** (D1234): a counted edict.
- **Underworld Breach** (D1235): escape granted to a graveyard; `escapeOf` is now the one place
  escape is read.
- **Ragavan** (D1236): an impulse draw from another player's library, cast only.

## The gate

Five gates in a row failed with vitest's `Timeout calling "onTaskUpdate"`, every test passing. The
cause was `scripts.test.ts` alone: thousands of synchronous tests that vitest runs without an
event-loop turn, past the worker's 60-second RPC timeout. A file-level `afterEach` that yields every
hundred tests fixed it (D1227); `vitest.config.ts` now says to run each file alone first when the
error returns. One merge also committed conflict markers into `scriptTypes.ts` (both sessions
added fields at one place); it was caught by the typecheck before any push and fixed in a follow-up
commit, and the merge script now stops on any unresolved conflict.
