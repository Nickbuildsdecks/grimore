## What the engine learned since D1117

Board-watching triggers. Three events were offered only to the permanent they happened to, and now
the board can watch them:
- **Becomes tapped** (D1121): "Whenever a creature an opponent controls becomes tapped" (Gideon's
  Avenger, Verity Circle, Rhoda), "a Dwarf you control" (Magda), "a Mountain an opponent controls"
  (Lifeblood).
- **Leaves the battlefield** (D1121): "another creature you control" (Ninth Bridge Patrol), "without
  dying" (Imperial Cosmographer), "if it had counters on it" (Leader's Talent), tokens (Nadier's
  Nightblade).
- **Is turned face up** (D1123): Aphetto Runecaster, Aven Farseer, Pine Walker.

These share one filter: whose, what type or subtype, token, a counter, without dying, not attacking.
`raise` offers the event to the whole board only while something there watches for it. A tap is
raised for every land tapped for mana, so this keeps that cheap.

New events:
- **Becomes untapped** (D1124): inspired, Key to the City, Wake Thrasher. It is found by comparing
  the board before and after, so the untap step, untap effects and Seedborn Muse all raise it. A
  trigger from the untap step goes on the stack in the upkeep (CR 502.4).
- **Enchantment creature tokens** (D1124): the Theros gods' and inspired cards' tokens.

Other families:
- "Two or more creatures attack" and "you attack a player" (D1118).
- "Whenever a creature you control explores" (D1119).
- Cast watchers for a legendary spell, a spell with {X}, and a spell from exile (D1120).
- Graveyard arrivals (D1122): "into an opponent's graveyard" (The Haunt of Hightower), "another
  creature or artifact you control" (Marionette Apprentice), and "~ or another".

Rules fixes:
- **D1121 and D1123.** The plain "when ~ leaves the battlefield" and "when ~ is turned face up"
  matchers answered yes to any event they were offered. That was harmless while only the subject was
  asked. Each now asks for its own permanent, and a test pins this on a watched board.
