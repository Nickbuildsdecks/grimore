## What the engine learned since D1184

Triggers:
- **Sacrifice triggers** with "X or Y", nontoken, a land or a creature type (D1185).
- **"Is dealt damage"** for any creature, permanent or subtype, and for an equipped creature (D1186).
- **Playing a land** as an event: "whenever you play a land", "an opponent plays a land" (D1187).

Conditions and static abilities:
- **Nine more "as long as" conditions** (D1188): you drew this turn, you're the monarch, the most
  cards in hand, it entered this turn, creature cards in a graveyard, exactly N. 35 cards in one
  step.
- **The Ward Auras' protection that spares its source** (D1189) and **protection from the chosen
  colour** (D1190).

Counts and measures:
- **A dying creature's counters**, moved to a target or counted (D1191): Dockworker Drone.
- **Compound measures** (D1193): "2 plus the number of …", "twice …", "A plus B".
- **"Cards named ~ in all graveyards / your graveyard"** (D1194): Kindle, Accumulated Knowledge.
- **"Create a number of … tokens equal to …"** and **the source's own power** (D1197): Evangel of
  Heliod, Master of Waves, Jacked Rabbit, Termagant Swarm. 16 cards in one step.
- **"The amount of life you gained this turn"** (D1198).

Questions:
- **The dig's consolation** (D1192): "if you didn't put a card into your hand this way".
- **A discard of a count** (D1195): "a card for each Swamp you control", "X cards". Underneath, a
  question the card works out as it is asked now also sees the resolution's own controller,
  targets and X.
- **"That player discards that many cards"** (D1196), and a fix found on the way: a computed
  question about "that player" was left aimed at a target nobody chose and asked nobody. It is now
  re-aimed like every other question.
