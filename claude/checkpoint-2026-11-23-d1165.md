## What the engine learned since D1159

Dungeons and the initiative:
- **The initiative and Undercity** (D1160). The Initiative and the dungeon are cards in the command
  zone. A venture asks which room when two are below. The holder ventures again at their upkeep,
  and combat damage to the holder takes the initiative. Each room's ability goes on the stack, and
  the bottom room completes the dungeon.
- **Venture into the dungeon** (D1163): Lost Mine of Phandelver, Dungeon of the Mad Mage and Tomb of
  Annihilation, every room read. Runestone Caverns' "you may play them" is a new permission with no
  deadline; Mad Wizard's Lair draws three and offers one as a free cast. "As long as you've
  completed a dungeon" is a condition.
- **"Whenever you complete a dungeon"** (D1164), including from the graveyard (Dungeon Crawler).

Effects:
- **"Until your next turn"** (D1161) on any continuous effect a clause makes, and "can't attack [or
  block] this turn".
- **A punisher, once** (D1162): "each player / each opponent / target player loses N life unless
  they discard a card / sacrifice …". The other choice is offered only to a player who can make it.
- **"For each opponent"** (D1165) as a count, and "For each opponent, …" done once per opponent.
