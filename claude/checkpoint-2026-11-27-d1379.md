## This stretch

- **The escape Titans** (D1370, D1371): "sacrifice it unless it escaped" (Uro, Kroxa, Phlage), and
  Kroxa's "each opponent discards a card, then each opponent who didn't discard a nonland card
  this way loses 3 life".
- **Mill, then you may take one** (D1372): "Mill three cards. You may put a land card from among
  them into your hand. If you don't, …" (Ainok Wayfarer, Ravenous Gigamole).
- **Narset, Parter of Veils** (D1373): a look at the top whose reveal is "a noncreature,
  nonland card".
- **Tribute** (D1374, CR 702.104): an opponent chooses whether the creature enters with the
  counters, and "if tribute wasn't paid" reads the answer.
- **Targets with different controllers** (D1375): "for each opponent, … up to one target … that
  player controls". This also fixed a D1356 gap in the bot's trigger aiming.
- **An Equipment that makes its own body** (D1376): "When ~ enters, create a 1/1 … token, then
  attach ~ to it", and its "you may pay {N}. If you do" form.
- **The Modern Horizons 2 Incarnations** (D1377): evoke paid by exiling a card of a colour, and
  Solitude's Swords to Plowshares trigger. Grief and Solitude read whole.
- **Divided damage on abilities** (D1378): triggered and activated abilities divide a quantity
  among their targets as spells have since #119. Fury, Inferno Titan, Bogardan Hellkite, Gang of
  Devils, Mogg Mob, Ral, Caller of Storms. The table collects the division one tap per point, and
  the bot takes the split that kills the most opposing creatures.
- **"You may pay {X}. If you do"** (D1379): X offered up to what the seat can pay, and paid by
  the engine. Squealing Devil, Flameblast Dragon, Rise of the Hobgoblins.
