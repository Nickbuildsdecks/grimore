## What the engine learned since D1124

Combat and keywords:
- Attached creatures' combat lines (D1125), keyword-filtered anthems and player hexproof (D1126).
- "Can't attack or block alone" (D1128).
- Assigns no combat damage (D1137), firebending and mana kept until end of combat (D1138).
- **Decayed** (D1143): can't block, sacrificed at end of combat after attacking.

Turn structure and costs:
- Extra turns (D1132).
- Exhaust and "activate only once" (D1133), boast (D1134).
- Bloodrush and renew (D1135), suspend's self-exile with time counters (D1130).

Mana:
- Mana equal to a power (D1139).
- "X mana of any one color, where X is …" (D1140).

Effects:
- Life-gain replacements (D1127).
- Bolster and chosen counters (D1129).
- Durationless grants (D1131).
- Domain, formidable and cohort (D1136).
- Targeted connive (D1141).
- **"Choose a creature type." spells** (D1142): the type is asked as the spell resolves.
- "You create …" read as "Create …" (D1143).

**The Ring tempts you** (D1144, D1145), on 53 legal cards:
- **The Ring as an emblem.** Each player gets the Ring as an emblem, with a Ring-bearer and a
  count of temptations.
- **The Ring's four abilities.**
  - Level 1: the Ring-bearer is legendary and has skulk's blocking rule.
  - Level 2: it loots when it attacks.
  - Level 3: a creature that blocks it is sacrificed at end of combat.
  - Level 4: its combat damage to a player costs each opponent 3 more life.
- **The triggers.** "Whenever the Ring tempts you" triggers, and "if you chose a creature other
  than ~".

Reader fix (D1145): a card whose name has a comma ("Gandalf, Friend of the Shire") failed in every
"When …, if …, …" trigger that named it. The name is now masked before the split.
