## What the engine learned since D1165

Effects and timing:
- **Delayed sacrifice, exile or destruction** at the next end step, of the source or a target, and
  a token's Oxford-comma keywords (D1166).
- **Graveyard card filters** with colours joined by "or", and comma lists with "legendary" (D1167).
- **Domain** as a static count, and every line retried without its ability word (D1168).

Scopes and counts:
- **Colours, keywords and counters on a mass scope** (D1169): "Destroy all green creatures",
  "creatures with flying", "with a +1/+1 counter on it", and the same on mass pumps.
- **Another player's count** (D1170): "they control", "in their graveyard", "defending player
  controls"; "for each creature blocking it"; "target opponent loses 1 life for each …".
- **That spell's mana value, and the mana spent to cast it** (D1171), for cast triggers and for
  counterspells. A countered spell's measures are kept for the rest of the turn.
- **X/X tokens** where X is any measure (D1172).
- **Sacrifice "a creature or artifact", "a token", "a Vehicle"** (D1173).
