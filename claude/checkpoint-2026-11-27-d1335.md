## This stretch

- **Ward paid by discarding** (D1327): "Ward—Discard a card". The player chooses the card on the
  table, and the bot gives up its cheapest.
- **Morph by revealing a colour** (D1328): "Morph—Reveal a black card in your hand".
- **The Borderposts** (D1329): the alternative cost that returns a basic land to hand.
- **"Discard two cards" as a cost** (D1330): the player chooses each card in turn, and the web table
  collects them one tap at a time.
- **Lion's Eye Diamond** (D1331): "Discard your hand" as a cost. The auto-payer never uses it; the
  player presses it.
- **"Sacrifice any number of …, then … that many"** (D1332): Last-Ditch Effort, Landslide,
  Malevolent Witchkite, God-Eternal Bontu's enter trigger. The bot sacrifices nothing to these.
- **Scapeshift** (D1333): a question worked out as it is asked now sees the answers given before
  it. The search takes as many lands as were sacrificed and no more.
- **Two engine faults found by sweep 96001** (D1334, D1335), below.

Seed 96041 threw "that card is not in your hand" on turn 53. A worktree at the D1326 build
replayed it. Two faults were behind it, each found once the one before it was fixed:
1. **A land offered as a cast** (D1334). A Lurrus-like "cast one permanent spell with mana value 2
   or less from your graveyard" fits a Mountain, so the land branch offered it as a land drop,
   which the engine then refused. A land is never cast; the branch now skips those offers.
2. **A free shield pressed a thousand times** (D1335). Nomads en-Kor's {0} redirect carried no bot
   hint, and neither did the Circles of Protection. Both are now marked as damage prevention, so
   the bot spends them only while damage is coming.

With both fixes on the D1326 build, seed 96041 finishes on turn 64. On the current build the same
seed finished on turn 52, because the cards read since D1326 play the game differently.
