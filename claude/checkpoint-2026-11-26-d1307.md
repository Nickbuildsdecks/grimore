## This stretch

- **Counters you put** (D1300): the engine now records who put each counter, so "whenever you put
  one or more counters on a permanent" works, as do its planeswalker, opponent and that-many forms.
  Infect and wither damage now announce their -1/-1 counters, which counter triggers had missed.
- **"Do this only once each turn"** (D1301): an optional trigger with this rider runs once a turn.
- **Removing counters as an effect** (D1302, D1303): a number of counters, all of them, or every kind,
  from this permanent, from a target, or from "it".
- **Exiling chosen cards from your graveyard** (D1304), with the rule that an "If you do" after a
  "you may exile N" needs all N.
- **Room doors on the web table** (D1305): each door shows as unlocked or locked.
- **Two mana of any one color, granted to a land** (D1306): Gift of Paradise and its kin. This
  exposed two places that pay for a land as a whole:
  - the auto-payer could make one mana from a land that makes two;
  - the reachable-mana count added a land's own mana to its granted mana.
  Both are fixed.
- **Storage lands and mana batteries** (D1307): "Remove X / any number of counters" as a cost, with
  the X announced. Seven storage lands, the five batteries and Kyren Toy play, along with Infused
  Arrows, Cruel Sadist, Chamber Sentry, Skullmane Baku, Arcbound Javelineer and Auntie Blyte. How
  many counters to remove is the player's choice, so the auto-payer never uses them. Five storage
  lands that charge only while kept tapped (Dwarven Hold, Bottomless Vault) are held back until
  keeping a land tapped is a real choice for its controller. That is next.
