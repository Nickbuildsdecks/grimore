  may play that card without paying its mana cost" is played for nothing. "Shuffle your library"
  reads on its own.

  - a fault from D1183 fixed: a doubly escaped regex had stopped a count in digits reading.

  reveal now also reads "you gain" and "each opponent loses".

  questions, each asked only while the answer before it was to go on.

  control that share a creature type with it".
- **Approach of the Second Sun** (D1349): spells cast this game are remembered by name. A resolving
  spell goes to the graveyard only if it is still on the stack (CR 608.2n).
- **Scroll Rack** (D1350).
- **Flash** (D1351): the price is worked out from the creature put in. A fault from D1333 fixed:
  the payment of a priced answer, its fallback and the bot's preferred answer now see the answers
