## What the engine learned since D1216

Triggers and referents:
- **An Aura that exiles its host, and "target opponent gains control of ~"** (D1217).
- **"That spell's controller" on a targeted trigger, and "a spell or ability you control"**
  (D1220): Bonecrusher Giant, and nine Valiant cards (Heartfire Hero, Emberheart Challenger, Mouse
  Trapper and others).
- **"Those creatures", "they" and "them" after a clause that aimed at several** (D1223): Sparkmage's
  Gambit, Wrap in Flames, Coronation of Chaos, Leo's Guidance.

Sentences:
- **"Look at target player's hand and choose N cards from it"** (D1221): Extortion, Thrull Surgeon,
  Painful Memories, Agonizing Memories.
- **Counters of two or three kinds in one sentence** (D1224): Unexpected Fangs, Gift of the Viper,
  Qarsi Revenant.

Engine additions:
- **Damage prevented by a kind of source** (D1218), and **one-turn combat prevention by any group**
  (D1219): Harmless Assault, Tanglesap, Vine Snare.
- **A delayed trigger on a death** (D1222): "When that creature dies this turn, …". Delayed
  triggers had only fired at a step. A watch now fires when the creature it was made about dies,
  only for that object (a blinked creature is a new one), and is dropped when the turn ends. A Good
  Day to Die, Searing Blood, Virulent Wound, Make Your Mark and five more.
