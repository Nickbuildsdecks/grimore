## What the engine learned since D1145

Targets and choices:
- "Choose target creature" as its own clause (D1146).
- Goad, of a target or of every creature in a scope (D1149).

Damage:
- Damage to the permanent itself replaced by counters, in five printed shapes (D1147).
- "Whenever you attack while …" read as an intervening-if (D1148).

Tokens and bodies:
- Amass, with the Army's subtype (D1150).
- Populate (D1151).
- Manifest, and turning a manifested creature card up for its mana cost (D1153).
- Incubate and the Incubator token (D1155).

Chance:
- Coin flips, with both branches and the result in the log (D1152).
- d20 tables, read as one effect, with the roll in the log (D1154).

Murders at Karlov Manor's mechanics:
- **Suspect** (D1156): menace and can't block until the creature leaves the battlefield. "Suspect
  it" after a graveyard target suspects the returned card, not the spell.
- **"If it's (not) suspected"** and a general **"If …, A. Otherwise, B."** (D1157).
- **Collect evidence** (D1158): as an ability cost, and as "you may collect evidence N. If you do /
  When you do". The engine picks the cards, the biggest first.
- **"You may collect evidence N" as an additional cost** (D1159), on the kicker's machinery, with
  "if evidence was collected".

Fix to D1153: a manifested card that left the battlefield kept its flag. `relocate` now clears
`manifested` and `suspected`.
