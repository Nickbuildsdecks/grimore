## What the engine learned in this run

Card families:
- **Auras and kickers.** Wider Aura hosts; kicked halves that refer back to the base; kickers paid
  by sacrifice or life.
- **Grants and taxes.** Abilities granted to artifacts and enchantments (Kataki); life-priced
  upkeep taxes and taxes that tap.
- **Mana.** "One mana of any type that land produced" (Mana Flare, Zendikar Resurgent, Mirari's
  Wake).
- **Wording.** Cards that name themselves in the plural; "player or battle" wordings while battles
  wait on the import.
- **Batches.** "Whenever one or more … enter", once per batch, and "that many" off a batch.
- **Entries and deaths** named with a colour, "legendary", a token or a mana value.
- **Casts.** "Your first instant or sorcery spell each turn".
- **Damage.** A player dealt damage by any source, burn spells included (Niv-Mizzet, Visionary).
- **Graveyards.** "Put into a graveyard from anywhere": the Eldrazi titans' shuffle, and the
  Blightsteel / Darksteel / Progenitus replacement.
- **Once a turn.** "For the first time each turn" as the once-a-turn restriction.

Rules fixes found by sweeps and tests:
- **D1113 (CR 603.10a).** A creature dying in the same wipe as others now sees them die. Blood
  Artist in a Wrath drains for every creature, not only itself.
- **D1109.** The bot no longer presses a free animation (Chimeric Idol) a thousand times.
- **D1115.** "One or two target creatures" is offered only when one exists. The bot had cast Wind
  Sail at nothing.
