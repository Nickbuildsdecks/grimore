## Commander staples this stretch

Most of these are cards a Commander table meets often, and the engine could not run them before:

- **Pithing Needle, Phyrexian Revoker** (D1237): a card name chosen as it enters. The question offers
  only names a player could know.
- **Meddling Mage, Nevermore, Voidstone Gargoyle** (D1238), on the same chosen name.
- **Rest in Peace, Leyline of the Void, Dryad Militant, and the "a creature an opponent controls
  would die" family** (D1239). A token-branch fix came with it: "your graveyard" now asks whose
  token it was.
- **Sorcerous Spyglass** (D1240): the name question also offers the hand it looked at.
- **Grafdigger's Cage, Kunoros, Weathered Runestone, Containment Priest** (D1241). Asked where a card
  changes zone, so no reanimation path can miss them. Flashback and escape are refused under a Cage.
- **Kalitas, The Darkness Crystal, Head of the Hunt, Nemata** (D1242): the exile and something done
  as well.
- **Tergrid, God of Fright** (D1243, D1244), both faces. A sacrifice trigger now carries the
  sacrificed permanent as its subject, and a punisher has a third way out.
- **Orcish Bowmasters** (D1245): not the draw step's own card.
- **Blood Moon, Magus of the Moon, Spreading Seas, Song of the Dryads** (D1246): a land's land types
  set (CR 305.7). Two fixes came with it: the mana pips now count a type an effect added (they had
  missed Urborg's), and a permanent's lost abilities are refused when activated, not only left off
  the play list.
- **Demonic Consultation** (D1247): a name from the caster's own library.

Not done, and why:
- Imprisoned in the Moon: a grant after the loss, on the attachment path.
- Ad Nauseam and Tainted Pact: a loop with a question each time round.
- Gilded Drake: exchanging control.
- Opposition Agent: controlling another player while they search.
