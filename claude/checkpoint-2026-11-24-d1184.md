## What the engine learned since D1173

Scopes and counts:
- **Edicts of any description** (D1174): "each opponent sacrifices a creature token", "… an
  artifact or creature".
- **Tokens in a scope** (D1175): "destroy all creature tokens", "for each token you control".
- **Counters on each permanent of any scope** (D1176), Blech's subtype list included.
- **Tokens in a static count** (D1177) and **anthems with a count** (D1178): Call for Unity,
  Joraga Warcaller, Thorin.

Sentences and conditions:
- **A sentence that starts with "Then"** (D1179): sentences already happen in order.
- **A trailing "unless" over a condition** (D1180): Chart a Course.
- **More conditions** (D1181): coven, a full party, "an artifact and an enchantment", "a Desert or
  a Desert card in your graveyard", "another creature with power 4 or greater".

Durations and combat:
- **"For as long as ~ remains tapped / you control ~"** on any continuous effect (D1182): Tawnos's
  Weaponry, Mana Leech, the Couriers, Dungeon Geists. A new engine field, `lastsWhile`, ends the
  effect the first time its terms fail.
- **Tokens that enter tapped and attacking** (D1183): Hero of Bladehold.
- **"Whenever a nontoken creature you control attacks"**, and the end-of-combat token rider
  (D1184): Militia's Pride, Mardu Ascendancy.
