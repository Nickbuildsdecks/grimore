## This stretch

- **Endure N** (D1317): +1/+1 counters or an N/N Spirit, the controller's choice. Only the Spirit
  if the creature has left.
- **Doran** (D1318): combat damage equal to toughness, for every creature or for yours. Every damage
  assignment in combat now asks one function.
- **Adamant** (D1319): the engine records how much of each mana type paid for a spell. This works
  on spells, on "enters with" counters and on enters triggers.
- **The Clockworks** (D1320): a +1/+1 counter removed at end of combat.
- **Frostwielder and Kumano** (D1321): a creature they damaged this turn is exiled instead of
  dying.
- **The Gustcloaks** (D1322): "you may untap it and remove it from combat".
- **Kozilek's Pathfinder** (D1323): "target creature can't block ~ this turn".
- **Three board conditions** (D1324): a card type in your graveyard, an opponent with no basic
  lands, and an opponent with a multicolored permanent.
- **The en-Kor redirects** (D1325): the next N damage to one creature is dealt to another.
- **A source of your choice** (D1326): the ten Circles of Protection and their kin. A new question
  offers every permanent and the spells on the stack, and a shield takes all of that source's next
  damage. The web table lets a player tap a spell on the stack to answer, and the bot names the
  spell it was activated against.
