## This stretch

- **A hand question beside a second target** (D1336): Aggressive Negotiations. A spell that asks
  the target opponent a question may also have a second target, when no later target could be a
  player.
- **Remand and Memory Lapse** (D1337): a countered spell can go to its owner's hand or the top of
  its library. "Return target spell to its owner's hand" is a bounce rather than a counter, so
  "can't be countered" does not stop it.
- **Hullbreaker Horror** (D1338): "choose up to one" on a trigger, with "Choose no mode" as the last
  answer.
- **A target that is a spell or a permanent** (D1339): a new target kind, for Venser, Shaper Savant,
  Unsubstantiate, Brutal Expulsion and Jeskai Revelation.
- **Intuition and Gifts Ungiven** (D1340): the caster searches, the cards are revealed, and the
  opponent chooses where they go. A question can now require cards with different names.
- **Isochron Scepter and Spellbinder** (D1341): a copy of the imprinted card, cast for nothing.
- **Mana as much as what was sacrificed** (D1342): Priest of Yawgmoth, Red Priest, Illuminor
  Szeras, and the spell Sacrifice.
- **Sacrifice costs by colour and narrower kind** (D1343): "a red or white creature", "a nontoken
  creature", "a noncreature artifact", "a nonland permanent" (Magmaw).
