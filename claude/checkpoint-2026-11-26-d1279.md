## This stretch

- **Counters** (D1270 to D1273):
  - Power/toughness counters of every size (+2/+2, -0/-1 …) now size the creature. On the way, the
    rule that keeps counters off a card that has left the battlefield was widened to cover them.
  - "That many" counters, with a batch of discards now carrying its count.
  - Two counters in one sentence.
  - Counters "for each", of any kind.
- **Pyroblast and Hydroblast** (D1274), Burnout and the Ring cycle: "… if it's blue", asked as the
  spell resolves.

  bot's move list stays fast with ten mode combinations and twelve creatures on the table.
- **Finality counters** (D1276): the rule itself, which nothing applied before, and the readers for
  returns with one.
