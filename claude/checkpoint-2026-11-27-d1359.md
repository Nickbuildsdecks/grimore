## This stretch

- **Protean Hulk** (D1352): a library search bounded by the total mana value of the cards found.
- **Graveyard returns of several cards with no target** (D1353): "return up to N creature cards
  from your graveyard to the battlefield", with or without a total.
- **Targets bounded by their total mana value** (D1354): Patch Up's "up to three target permanent
  cards with total mana value 6 or less", checked when the spell is cast and honoured by the bot.
- **"Put a counter on each of them"** (D1355), after a sentence that aimed at several.
- **"Any number of target …"** (D1356): about 160 printed lines, read through the target-phrase
  quantifiers. The bot takes what is on the same side as its first pick, so "destroy any number of
  target creatures" stops before its own. March from the Tomb, Scapegoat, Bone Harvest.
- **Strive** (D1357): a coloured charge for each target beyond the first. Ten strive cards read
  whole, and Fireball's own cost line now reads.
- **X damage divided as you choose** (D1358): Rolling Thunder, Hail of Arrows, Rock Slide. The
  division check, the bot and the web table all read the X announced.
- **Tokens for what any number of targets destroyed or exiled** (D1359): Descent of the Dragons,
  Hour of Need.
